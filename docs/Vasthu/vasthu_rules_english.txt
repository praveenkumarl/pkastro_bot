Section 1: Vastu and Housing Rules
[Category: Vastu | Keywords: Debilitated Planet (Neecha), Combustion (Asthamana), 4th House]
If a planet is debilitated or combust in the 4th house, there will be a Vastu defect in the house in the direction associated with that specific planet.

[Category: Vastu | Keywords: Rahu, 6-8-12 Lords, Thithi Sunyam, Pathakam]
If a debilitated planet, a combust planet, or Rahu is in the 4th house, and the 4th lord is placed in the 6th, 8th, or 12th houses with Pathakam, Thithi Sunyam, or Mandi connection, the house will have significant Vastu defects.

[Category: Vastu | Keywords: Mandi, Ketu, Pathakathipathi]
If Ketu or Mandi is in the 4th house along with the Pathakathipathi (lord of the obstacle house), and that lord is placed in the 6th, 8th, or 12th houses, the house will have Vastu flaws.

[Category: Housing | Keywords: Terraced House (Maadi Veedu), Sun, 11th-12th Lords]
If the Sun is in the 4th house or the 4th lord is in the 11th or 12th houses, the person will live in a terraced house. If there is also a Mars connection, a multi-story or terraced house is guaranteed.

[Category: Housing | Keywords: Apartment, Retrograde (Vakkira), Rahu-Ketu, Saturn]
If the 4th lord is retrograde or a retrograde planet is in the 4th house with Rahu/Ketu and Saturn connections, the person may sell a portion of the property or rent it out. If the Sun is also involved in this combination, they will live in an apartment.

[Category: Vastu | Keywords: Temple Vicinity, 5th-9th Lords, Jupiter, Ketu]
If the Sun, Jupiter, Ketu, or the 5th or 9th lords are in the 4th house, the person will live near a temple. Venus and Mercury in the 4th indicate living near a Perumal (Vishnu) temple, while the Moon indicates an Amman temple.

Section 2: Property and Wealth
[Category: Property | Keywords: Earth Signs, Mars, Taurus-Virgo-Capricorn]
If the 4th lord or Mars is in the earth signs (Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn) without Pathakam or Thithi Sunyam connections, the person will acquire property even if they were born without any.

[Category: Property | Keywords: Trines (Trikona), 1st-4th-5th-9th Houses]
If the 4th lord or Mars is in trine houses (1, 4, 5, 9) without Thithi Sunyam or Pathakam connections, property ownership is certain.

[Category: Property | Keywords: Family Property, 2nd House Lord]
If the 4th lord is in the 2nd house without Thithi Sunyam or Pathakam connections, the person will inherit or gain family property.

[Category: Property Ownership | Keywords: Father's Name, Sun, 9th Lord]
A Sun-Mars conjunction or the 9th lord in the 4th house indicates that property will be held in the father's name.

[Category: Property Ownership | Keywords: Mother's Name, Moon, 4th Lord]
A Moon-Mars conjunction or the 4th lord remaining in the 4th house indicates property will be in the mother's name.

[Category: Property Ownership | Keywords: Wife's Name, Venus, 7th Lord]
A Venus-Mars conjunction or the 7th lord in the 4th house indicates property will be in the wife's name.

Section 3: Vehicles and Luxury
[Category: Vehicles | Keywords: New Car, Venus, 4th Lord, 11th Lord]
If the 4th house or 4th lord is in the 1st, 2nd, 4th, 5th, 9th, 10th, or 11th houses, a vehicle will be acquired. A connection with Venus specifically indicates a car.

[Category: Vehicles | Keywords: Trucks/Lorries, Saturn, Mars, Moon]
A connection between the 4th house/lord and Saturn or Mars indicates freight vehicles like lorries. If the Moon is also involved, it indicates load-carrying vehicles.

[Category: Vehicles | Keywords: Luxury Car, Jupiter, 5th-9th Lords]
If the 4th lord is connected to the 5th, 9th, or 11th lords, or to Jupiter, Venus, Mercury, or the Sun, the person will own a luxury car. A Jupiter-Venus-4th lord connection indicates a very expensive, large luxury car.

[Category: Vehicles | Keywords: Old/Used Car, Saturn, 8th Lord, Waning Moon]
A connection between the 4th house/lord and Saturn, the 8th lord, or a waning Moon indicates the purchase of a second-hand or old car.

[Category: Vehicles | Keywords: Vehicle Color, Planetary Rulership]
The vehicle's color is determined by the planet ruling the star (nakshatra) of the 4th lord: Moon/Venus (White), Sun (Orange), Mars (Red), Jupiter (Yellow), Mercury (Green), Saturn/Rahu (Black/Blue), Ketu (Multi-colored).

Section 4: Education and Career
[Category: Education | Keywords: Hurdles, 6-8-12 Houses, Combustion]
Connections between the 4th house and the 6th, 8th, or 12th houses, or the 4th lord being combust or having Pathakam connection, cause hurdles in education or average grades.

[Category: Education | Keywords: Diploma, Mars-Saturn, 3rd-5th Houses]
If the 4th house is weak but the 3rd and 5th houses are strong, or if there are Mars-Saturn-Ketu connections to the 4th, the person is likely to pursue diploma-level education.

[Category: Career | Keywords: Government Job, Sun, Jupiter, Aries-Leo-Scorpio]
If the 4th lord is in the Lagna or 4th house with Sun, Jupiter, or Mars connections in signs like Aries, Leo, or Scorpio, the person will hold a government position.

[Category: Career | Keywords: Private Sector, Saturn, Rahu, 4th House]
If the 4th house or its lord is connected to Saturn or Rahu, the person will hold a position in the private sector.

Section 5: Nadi Gochara Phalam (Planetary Transits)
[Category: Transit (Gochara) | Planets: Sun (Suriyan) over Planets]


Sun over Sun: Success in endeavors and physical strength.


Sun over Moon: Change of residence and travel for financial needs.


Sun over Mars: Blood pressure, fever, and mental stress.


Sun over Mercury: Help from father's friends and son's interest in education.


Sun over Jupiter: Career promotion, government support, and birth of a male child.

[Category: Transit (Gochara) | Planets: Moon (Chandran) over Planets]


Moon over Sun: Disrepute for the father and potential travel for him.


Moon over Moon: Interactions with the opposite sex and mental confusion.


Moon over Mars: Injury to the son and physical weakness for the mother.


Moon over Jupiter: Uterine issues for women and humiliation through women.

[Category: Transit (Gochara) | Planets: Saturn (Sani) over Planets]


Saturn over Sun: Father's ill health and trouble from government officials.


Saturn over Moon: Mother's ill health, disrepute, and dissatisfaction in work.


Saturn over Jupiter: Employment opportunities, promotion, and financial gain.


Saturn over Venus: Purchase of immovable property or vehicles.

Section 6: Health, Accidents, and Peace
[Category: Mental Peace | Keywords: 4th Lord, Moon, 1-5-9 Houses]
If the 4th lord or the Moon is in the 1st, 5th, or 9th houses without 6-8-12 or Pathakam connections, the person will enjoy mental peace.

[Category: Health | Keywords: 4th House, 5th-9th Lords, Recovery]
If the 5th or 9th lords are in the 4th house without being afflicted, the person will recover from illnesses. Worshiping the family deity (Kula Deivam) during illness is beneficial.

[Category: Accidents | Keywords: 12th House, Saturn, Bathroom/Stairs]
If Saturn is in the 12th from Lagna or Lagna lord and connects with the 4th house, accidents at home may occur. A Saturn-Moon connection indicates slipping in the bathroom, while Saturn-Sun indicates falling from stairs.

Section 7: Specific Property Obstacles
[Category: Debt/Loss | Keywords: Property Seizure, Government, Rahu, Sun]
If the 4th lord is in the 6th, 8th, or 12th, or is debilitated/combust and connects with the Sun or Rahu and the Pathakathipathi, the government may seize the property.

[Category: Debt/Loss | Keywords: Mortgaged Property, 6th-12th Houses, Mercury]
A 4-6-10-12-Pathakam connection indicates that property mortgaged for business loans may be lost due to inability to repay the debt.
