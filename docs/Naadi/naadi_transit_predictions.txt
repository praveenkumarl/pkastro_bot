Nadi Gochara Phalam: Complete Knowledge Base
Transits over the Sun (சூரியன்):

When the Sun transits over the Sun: It results in success in tasks and physical strength.

When the Moon transits over the Sun: It causes a bad reputation for the father and father's travel.

When Mars transits over the Sun: It leads to blood pressure, fever, mental stress, and fatigue.

When Mercury transits over the Sun: The father receives help from friends, and the son gains interest in education; it also increases land revenue for the father.

When Jupiter transits over the Sun: It brings promotion, government support, honor, and spiritual involvement; it may also signify the birth of a male child.

When Venus transits over the Sun: It results in financial gain for the father.

When Saturn transits over the Sun: It causes the father to lose health, trouble from government officials, and a struggle in business; it can also cause conflict between father and son.

When Rahu transits over the Sun: It indicates health issues for the father or son, laziness, and bad news.

When Ketu transits over the Sun: It results in health issues for the father or son, spiritual connection, and the father going on a holy pilgrimage.

Transits over the Moon (சந்திரன்):

When the Sun transits over the Moon: It causes a change of residence and the father to travel for financial needs.

When the Moon transits over the Moon: It brings mental confusion and interactions with the opposite sex.

When Mars transits over the Moon: It leads to mental agony, accidents, and travel for the brother or husband.

When Mercury transits over the Moon: It gives motherly mental strength.

When Jupiter transits over the Moon: It can lead to change of place, health issues due to a fast-paced life, or trouble from hidden enemies.

When Venus transits over the Moon: It causes illness for the wife and financial waste.

When Saturn transits over the Moon: It signifies mother's health issues, loss of reputation, or professional dissatisfaction.

When Rahu transits over the Moon: It indicates mother's health issues, fear, mental distress, and trouble from black magic or voodoo.

When Ketu transits over the Moon: It causes mother's health issues, spiritual involvement, and bathing in holy rivers.

Transits over Mars (செவ்வாய்):

When the Sun transits over Mars: It leads to blood-related disorders and menstrual problems.

When the Moon transits over Mars: It causes mental agony, accidents, and travel for the brother or husband.

When Mars transits over Mars: It brings accidents, high physical energy or labor, and trouble from enemies.

When Mercury transits over Mars: It leads to an increase in body heat, blood pressure, danger from fire or weapons, and shoulder pain; it may also cause a job transfer for the husband.

When Jupiter transits over Mars: It indicates marriage for women, but may cause high blood pressure; it can also cause vain arguments for a brother or husband.

When Venus transits over Mars: It brings benefits for the brother, building a house, and wife's pregnancy.

When Saturn transits over Mars: It brings loss to a brother or husband, delayed promotions, and a lack of mental peace.

When Rahu transits over Mars: It is associated with vehicle accidents, cut wounds, premature birth, and brother's health issues.

When Ketu transits over Mars: It causes high blood pressure, nerve weakness, and differences or separation between husband and wife.

Transits over Mercury (புதன்):

When the Sun transits over Mercury: It results in financial gain for the father regarding land.

When the Moon transits over Mercury: It brings a bad reputation, travel for arts, and friendship with the opposite sex.

When Mars transits over Mercury: It causes disagreements with brothers or friends and skin diseases for the husband.

When Mercury transits over Mercury: It results in a high interest in learning education and arts.

When Jupiter transits over Mercury: It results in success in education, house construction, and buying land.

When Venus transits over Mercury: It leads to financial gain and income from land-related assets.

When Saturn transits over Mercury: It results in buying land or gardens, starting a new business, and success in education.

When Rahu transits over Mercury: It causes skin diseases, mental confusion, and the purchase of new vehicles.

When Ketu transits over Mercury: It causes land disputes, education hurdles, skin disease, and trade obstacles.

Transits over Jupiter (வியாழன்):

When the Sun transits over Jupiter: It brings profit in business for the father, respect, and friendship with scholars.

When the Moon transits over Jupiter: It leads to uterine disorders for women and humiliation caused by women.

When Mars transits over Jupiter: It brings an increase in income for the husband and contact with relatives.

When Mercury transits over Jupiter: It results in a high interest in education and arts, and learning scriptures.

When Jupiter transits over Jupiter: It results in fame, honor, spiritual growth, and the birth of a male child.

When Venus transits over Jupiter: It brings an increase in income but may cause disagreements in the family.

When Saturn transits over Jupiter: It is associated with getting a job, promotion, financial gain, and support from elders.

When Rahu transits over Jupiter: It signifies death of a relative, accidents, and mental confusion.

When Ketu transits over Jupiter: It causes laziness, spiritual interest, and hurdles in good events.

Transits over Venus (சுக்கிரன்):

When the Sun transits over Venus: It brings marriage opportunities, high libido, and health issues for the wife.

When the Moon transits over Venus: It leads to loss in business, travel to other towns, and interest in learning new things.

When Mars transits over Venus: It indicates an income increase, luck, and a happy mind.

When Mercury transits over Venus: It results in financial gain, buying assets, and auspicious events.

When Jupiter transits over Venus: It brings marriage for women, birth of a female child, and accumulation of assets.

When Venus transits over Venus: It results in financial gain, support from the wife, but the wife may suffer from joint pain.

When Saturn transits over Venus: It results in buying immovable property or vehicles and building a house.

When Rahu transits over Venus: It signifies illness for the wife or daughter.

When Ketu transits over Venus: It causes health issues for the wife or brother.

Transits over Saturn (சனி):

When the Sun transits over Saturn: It causes diseases and a financial crunch.

When the Moon transits over Saturn: It indicates vain expenses, debt, and contact with cheaters.

When Mars transits over Saturn: It causes a financial crisis, loss of self-confidence, and problems in business.

When Mercury transits over Saturn: It brings high profit in business and land-related income.

When Jupiter transits over Saturn: It leads to getting a job, promotion, and economic progress.

When Venus transits over Saturn: It results in auspicious events in the family, financial gain, and cooperation from the wife.

When Saturn transits over Saturn: It signifies troubles and changes in the profession.

When Rahu transits over Saturn: It is associated with the death of a relative, contact with bad people, and laziness.

When Ketu transits over Saturn: It causes hurdles in job promotions, professional stagnation, and interest in pilgrimages.

Transits over Rahu (ராகு):

When the Sun transits over Rahu: It indicates profit in gambling, lottery, or speculation, but sorrow due to children and trouble from the government.

When the Moon transits over Rahu: It signifies women playing an important role and intestinal diseases like diarrhea.

When Mars transits over Rahu: It leads to high libido, possible death of a brother or husband, and marriage hurdles.

When Mercury transits over Rahu: It brings an increase in speaking ability and favorable results.

When Jupiter transits over Rahu: It results in surgery, miscarriage, hurdles in education, or victory in legal cases.

When Venus transits over Rahu: It leads to auspicious results for the wife and marriage.

When Saturn transits over Rahu: It signifies the death of a relative, property gained through hard labor or theft, and poisonous bites.

When Rahu transits over Rahu: It gives average results depending on the planets associated with it.

When Ketu transits over Rahu: It causes stomach disorders and diarrhea.

Transits over Ketu (கேது):

When the Sun transits over Ketu: It results in a bad reputation and a state of unhappiness.

When the Moon transits over Ketu: It causes differences with parents and trouble from public contact.

When Mars transits over Ketu: It results in domineering thoughts.

When Mercury transits over Ketu: It causes disagreements with friends and no favorable results.

When Jupiter transits over Ketu: It results in poor health and a lack of mental peace.

When Venus transits over Ketu: It signifies a split in the family.

When Saturn transits over Ketu: It results in professional stagnation and laziness.

When Rahu transits over Ketu: It indicates hurdles for good results; specifically, a Venus-Ketu conjunction leads to loss of wealth.

When Ketu transits over Ketu: It causes a lack of unity and differences in the family.
